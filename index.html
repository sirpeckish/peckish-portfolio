I exterminate all common infestations

Every website, app and API has them. The only question is who finds them first: me, or somebody far less polite.

  • SQL and code injection

    Structural pest

    Eats straight through your data layer. Left untreated, brings the whole application down.

    name=' OR 1=1 --

  • Broken access control

    Door and lock specialist

    Your locks look fine, but half of them don't check who's holding the key. I test every door.

    GET /admin 200 OK

  • IDOR

    Gets into other people's data

    Change one number in a request, get another user's data. Astonishingly common; very quick to catch.

    id=41 id=42

  • Information disclosure

    Leaks and seepage

    Secrets, internals and credentials seeping out of the foundations, then chained into worse.

    GET /.env 200 OK

  • Cross-site scripting

    Swarms: stored, reflected, DOM

    One unescaped field and it's in every visitor's browser. I trace each swarm to demonstrated account impact. No alert(1) and gone.

    <img src=x onerror=…>
